# Hiring portfolio
A recruiter evaluating Juliano for senior backend and technical lead roles should be able to judge fit, read evidence and contact him in one visit.

- AC-01. The opening identifies Juliano, his focus and US work authorization using his brand identity.
- AC-02. A reader can connect at least two named projects to his own contributions, tools and outcomes.
- AC-03. Career history, skills, education, certifications and languages are accessible on the page.
- AC-04. A reader can download his current PDF and reach him by email, phone or LinkedIn.
- AC-05. Navigation, content and actions remain usable on narrow screens and by keyboard.

Use Resume.pdf from Drive for claims. LinkedIn blocked direct retrieval. Start dates, location and availability beyond the resume are not assumed.

- AC-06. Show the portrait and explicit U.S. Green Card status in the opening, with a smaller name heading.
- AC-07. Group technologies by engineering topic and connect them to professional project examples.

- AC-08. Keep the portrait to the left of the introduction on desktop and mobile. Describe community through documented mentoring, shared engineering practices and product collaboration. Omit personal projects and repository links pending explicit selection by Juliano. Use the achievements document and resume to distinguish individual contributions from employer-wide statistics.

- AC-09. Show measured project outcomes prominently. Include the ten organization-scale figures from the achievements document beside their corresponding projects with named entities, reporting periods where available, and original source links.
